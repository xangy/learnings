## 1. Ingress & Egress:
They are used to define the data flow into or out of the network, system, or software boundary.

ingress mean data flowing into the system.
egress mean data flowing out of the system.

As a dev, need to manage both ingress and egress but the priority might be different while dealing with them.

|Focus Area|Ingress Management|Egress Management|
|---|---|---|
|**Primary Goal**|Authentication, Routing, Rate Limiting|Cost Control, Performance, Third-Party Resilience|
|**Core Metric**|Request volume, Concurrent connections|Bandwidth volume (GB/s), Data payload size|
|**Biggest Risk**|Unauthorized Access, DDoS Attacks|High Cloud Bills, Data Leaks, System Slowdowns|
